The problem

Most job seekers can't see what separates the searches that get interviews from the ones that don't. We're interviewing job seekers to find out what's actually working right now, and then showing you where your search differs.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. We're asking for 15 minutes of your time. In exchange you get your report. We need baseline interviews to learn what's working in the market.

Who is this for?

Anyone actively job searching who isn't getting as many interviews with recruiters and hiring managers as they'd like. It works at any level and in any field.

How accurate is the ranking?

We're at an early stage. Scores describe job search behaviors, not your value as a candidate, and should be read as directional. As more interviews are completed, comparisons get more precise.

Do I need to prepare?

No. It helps to have a rough sense of where your recent interviews came from and the kinds of roles you've applied to, but you can answer from memory.

What do I need for the interview?

A device with a microphone and a quiet place for about 15 minutes. It's a voice conversation, not a typed form.

What happens with my answers?

Your answers are used to build the benchmark and to produce your report. You'll enter your email at the start of the interview so we can send it to you.
