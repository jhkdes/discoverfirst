Why it's worth reading

Five findings, each with what to do about it.

In this sample nearly everyone uses AI every day, so usage counts stopped telling PMs apart. These five patterns did.

  1. 28/32use AI several times a day

    Finding 01How well PMs use AI matters more than how often

    Frequency no longer sets PMs apart. Context and checking do. Sending long AI documents nobody has read costs the sender trust and puts the review on the reader.

  2. 19/32described a specific AI error

    Finding 02Once AI earns trust, PMs stop checking it

    Almost always a person who knew the domain caught the error, not the model. Another 12 couldn't name a failure at all, because fixing errors had become routine.

  3. 8/32describe a leadership AI story that runs ahead of reality

    Finding 03Mandates without support produce adoption theater

    Mandates arrive without training, connected tools or measurement. It shows up in every company-size band, and team culture and a local champion mattered more than size.

  4. 19/32won't hand AI live conversations or the call on what to build

    Finding 04PMs hold the line on real people and judgment

    No one uses synthetic users in place of real ones. Several see judgment as their edge, because AI makes everyone's output look the same.

  5. 21/32now prototype or build with AI

    Finding 05AI widens the PM's job and creates review work

    PMs take on legal, compliance, pricing and design work. The new job is reviewing output that is almost right but subtly off, and most of that work goes unseen.
