The problem

Hearing what former colleagues value in you is harder than it should be.

Colleagues are busy

Scheduling a live conversation is hard for everyone.

Asking takes courage

Writing and chasing each request yourself is uncomfortable.

General requests get general answers

"Any feedback for me?" rarely brings out a specific moment.

Your own view is only part of the picture

While you search, you want to hear the strengths other people recognize in you, not just your own assessment.

How it works

You add the names. We do the asking.

1

Add a colleague

Enter a first name and an email address for someone who worked with you.

2

Review the invitation

We send it on your behalf, with you on CC. You can edit it, and nothing goes out until you click Send.

3

They tell two stories

A voice conversation of 5 to 10 minutes with an AI interviewer, at a time that suits them. It is recorded, and they are told so before they start. We send two reminders.

4

You get their stories

Each colleague's stories appear on your results page, in their words and voice, plus a narrated recording.

What you get

Up to three stories from each colleague.

Each story is the colleague's own words, in order, with the reason they chose it and audio you can play.

01

Saw you at your best

A moment the colleague remembers you at your best, and why they picked it.

02

The moment they appreciated most

The time they were most glad to be working with you.

03

What they say you're especially good at

The strength in their own words, then the story that shows it best.

You also get a narrated recording of each colleague's stories. An AI narrator, disclosed as one, introduces each part, and the colleague's stories play in order.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The private beta is free.

What does "private beta" mean?

We aren't promoting it publicly yet. Anyone with this link can sign up, and we're learning from the first people who do.

Who should I ask?

Former colleagues who worked with you directly and who are willing to talk with an AI interviewer.

Is the conversation recorded?

Yes. Before they start, colleagues are told they are speaking with an AI and that the conversation is recorded. You receive the stories we build from it, in their voice, not the full interview.

Can colleagues stay anonymous?

No. Stories are in the colleague's own voice, so you can often tell who said what. We tell the colleague this up front.

What if a colleague only has one story?

The interviewer asks for two stories, but a colleague with one, or none, still has a complete conversation. Your results page says plainly which pieces they gave.

How long does it take them?

About 5 to 10 minutes. They don't need an account.

I typed the wrong email address. What now?

You can correct it in the review step before you send. After that, remove the colleague and add them again with the right address.

I got an invitation from Personal Feedback Agent for someone else. What is this?

A former colleague asked us to invite you to share a short story about working with them. Every email has a Stop link that ends all email from us to your address.
